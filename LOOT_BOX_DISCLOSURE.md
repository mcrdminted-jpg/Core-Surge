# Randomized Item Disclosure — Card Pull System

**Core Surge: Endless Tower Defense**

**Effective Date:** May 24, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 24, 2026

In compliance with Apple App Store Guidelines (Section 3.1.1) and Google Play Store Policies (Randomized Virtual Items), this document discloses the exact probabilities for all randomized virtual item acquisitions in Core Surge: Endless Tower Defense.

---

## 1. Card Pull Overview

The Game features a card collection system where players can spend Gems (in-game virtual currency) to perform randomized "card pulls." Each pull awards one card from the card pool.

### 1.1 Pull Costs

| Pull Type | Cost | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|
| Single Pull | 20 Gems | One random card |
| Bundle Pull (10x) | 180 Gems | Ten random cards (10% discount vs. singles) |

### 1.2 Gem Acquisition

Gems can be earned through gameplay (tournament rewards, milestone bonuses) or purchased with real money:

| Product | Price | Gems |
|---------|-------|------|
| Starter Pack (one-time) | $0.99 | 250 Gems + 2,500 Scrap + 1 Card |
| Gem Pack Small | $2.99 | 750 Gems |
| Gem Pack Medium | $9.99 | 2,800 Gems |
| Monthly Vault (subscription) | $9.99/mo | 500 Gems/month + Premium Rewards |

## 3. Duplicate Card Behavior

If you pull a card you already own, the duplicate copy counts toward leveling up that card. Cards have 5 levels, and the number of total copies required to reach each level varies by rarity tier.

### 3.1 Copies Required per Level

| Level | Standard | Prime | Apex |
|-------|----------|-------|------|
| Level 1 (Unlock) | 1 copy | 1 copy | 1 copy |
| Level 2 | 2 copies total | 2 copies total | 2 copies total |
| Level 3 | 4 copies total | 4 copies total | 4 copies total |
| Level 4 | 7 copies total | 8 copies total | 8 copies total |
| Level 5 (Max) | 12 copies total | 14 copies total | 16 copies total |

### 3.2 Duplicate Value

Every duplicate copy progresses you toward the next card level. Higher card levels provide stronger stat bonuses. Duplicates are never wasted; once a card reaches maximum level, further duplicates are not possible to obtain for that card.

## 4. Statistical Independence

### 4.1 No Pity System

Each card pull is statistically independent. Previous pull results do not affect future pull probabilities. There is no guaranteed rare card after a certain number of pulls.

### 4.2 No Hidden Modifiers

Drop rates are not modified by:
- Time of day or day of week
- Player spending history
- Account age or player level
- Number of consecutive pulls
- Any other hidden factor

The probabilities listed in this document are the exact values used by the game's random number generator.

## 5. Alternative Acquisition Methods

### 5.1 Direct Unlock (Non-Randomized)

Players may also directly unlock specific Standard and Prime tier cards without randomization:

| Tier | Direct Unlock Cost |
|------|--------------------|
| Standard | 60 Gems per card |
| Prime | 180 Gems per card |
| Apex | Not available for direct unlock |

Direct unlocks provide exactly one copy of the chosen card (Level 1). Additional copies for leveling must be obtained through card pulls.

### 5.2 Gameplay Rewards

Some cards or card copies may be awarded as tournament prizes or milestone rewards at no additional cost.
