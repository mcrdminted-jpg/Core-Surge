Promotional text can be updated at any time without submitting a new app version. Use this for seasonal events, updates, and marketing pushes.

### Launch
NEW! Command the ultimate tower defense. 25 cards, 18 tiers, endless strategy. Download and defend your Core today!

### Update 1 - Tournament Season
NEW UPDATE: Tournament Season 1 is live! Climb the ranks and earn exclusive rewards. How high can you place?

### Update 2 - Holiday Event
HOLIDAY EVENT: Double gems and special card packs available for a limited time! Don't miss out, Commander.

### Update 3 - New Cards
NEW CARDS ADDED: Expand your loadout with powerful new abilities. New meta, new strategies, new possibilities.

### Update 4 - Anniversary
ANNIVERSARY CELEBRATION: Special rewards, bonus gems, and a new exclusive skin. Thank you for one year of defense!

## Google Play Promotional Notes

Google Play does not have a dedicated promotional text field like Apple. Instead, update the short description (80 chars) or the first paragraph of the full description to reflect current promotions. The short description appears in search results and should always be compelling.

### Rotating Short Descriptions
- Default: Upgrade your Core, collect cards, and survive endless waves of enemies!
- Tournament: Tournament Season 1 is LIVE! Compete globally in Core Surge!
- Event: Limited-time event with double rewards! Play Core Surge now!
