## Full Description

One tower. Endless enemies. Infinite strategy.

Core Surge is the tower defense game built for players who want real depth. Command a single powerful Core tower against relentless waves of enemies. Every upgrade, every card, every decision shapes your path to dominance.

--- KEY FEATURES ---

ENDLESS WAVE COMBAT
- Battle against 12 unique enemy types with distinct behaviors
- Waves scale infinitely so every run pushes your limits
- Real-time combat with crits, multishot, bounce, and combo mechanics

25 COLLECTIBLE CARDS
- Collect 25 ability cards across 3 rarity tiers: Standard, Prime, and Apex
- Build custom loadouts that match your strategy
- Level up cards for increased power and new effects

6 UPGRADE FAMILIES
- Unlock permanent progression across 6 research paths
- Rank up 20+ stats that carry over between runs
- Specialize your Core into a unique powerhouse

18 DIFFICULTY TIERS
- Climb through 18 escalating challenge tiers
- Each tier brings tougher enemies and better rewards
- Only the most dedicated players reach Tier 18

TOURNAMENT SYSTEM
- Compete in global tournaments with 5 leagues per tier
- Climb seasonal leaderboards for exclusive rewards
- Prove your Core is the strongest in the world

7 COSMETIC SKINS
- Customize your tower with 7 unique visual themes
- Each skin features custom backgrounds and tower designs
- Stand out in tournaments with your favorite look

PLAY OFFLINE
- Full gameplay available without internet
- Cloud save available when you want it
- No forced ads interrupting your runs

--- WHY CORE SURGE? ---

Most tower defense games spread your attention across dozens of towers. Core Surge focuses everything on one tower and makes every strategic choice count. Your card loadout, your upgrade investments, and your in-battle decisions determine how far you survive.

Deep progression. Strategic combat. Endless replayability.

Download Core Surge and defend your tower today.
