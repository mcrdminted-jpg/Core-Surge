# Core Surge v0.7.23 Launch & Marketing Roadmap
**Target: Weeks 1-8 (Parallel to Build & Compliance) | Assigned: Launch Team**

## Overview
Core Surge v0.7.23 requires a coordinated launch strategy spanning beta testing, community building, app store optimization, influencer partnerships, and post-launch content cadence. Success depends on sustained player acquisition, retention, and engagement across multiple channels. Timeline overlaps with build and compliance tracks; launch readiness gates begin Week 6.

---

## Week 1-2: Beta Testing Infrastructure & Friend Group Launch

### Task 1.1 - Closed Beta Program Setup
- **Current state:** No beta infrastructure; single-player testing only
- **Goal:** Controlled beta with 50-100 engaged testers providing feedback before wider launch
- **Steps:**
  1. Set up TestFlight (iOS) and Google Play internal testing (Android)
  2. Create beta feedback form (Google Form or Typeform)
     - Questions: gameplay progression, card balance, difficulty curve, UI clarity, bug reports
     - Submission target: daily/weekly feedback cycles
  3. Create private Discord channel for beta testers (instant support, rapid iteration)
  4. Establish feedback triage process (daily review, prioritized bug fixes)
  5. Create TestFlight/Play Console invite list (target friends, content creators, gaming communities)
- **Deliverable:** Closed beta program live on both platforms; 50+ testers onboarded; feedback form integrated
- **Verification:** Daily feedback arriving; bugs triaged within 24 hours; at least 3 build iterations based on feedback

### Task 1.2 - Friend Group Launch (Week 2)
- **Current state:** Game exists but no player onboarding flow
- **Goal:** Soft launch to trusted group (50-100 friends/acquaintances) to gather early engagement data
- **Steps:**
  1. Prepare invite system (link sharing, referral tracking if possible)
  2. Create welcome guide/tutorial (first-time onboarding flow if not exists)
  3. Publish "Core Surge Beta - Help Shape the Game" announcement with 2-3 key features highlighted
  4. Share on personal social (Twitter, Reddit, Discord communities)
  5. Track day-1 retention, session length, card pulls to establish baseline metrics
- **Deliverable:** Game links shared; first wave of players acquired; baseline retention/DAU data collected
- **Verification:** 50-100 players in first week; >40% day-1 retention; session data flowing to analytics dashboard

### Task 1.3 - Community Infrastructure Kickoff
- **Current state:** No official community channels
- **Goal:** Establish Discord and Reddit as ongoing community hubs
- **Steps:**
  1. Create Discord server:
     - Channels: announcements, general-discussion, gameplay-help, bug-reports, lore-discussion, content-creator-program
     - Set bot (MEE6 or UnbelievaBoat for moderation, leveling)
     - Write welcome message + rules (no spam, toxicity, RMT)
  2. Create subreddit (r/CoreSurgeGame or similar):
     - Sidebar rules, daily megathread for questions
     - Stickied posts: patch notes, bug tracker, roadmap
     - Moderation team (self + 1-2 trusted players)
  3. Link Discord & Reddit in-game (settings menu, splash screens)
- **Deliverable:** Discord server 50+ members; subreddit created with 20+ subscribers; in-game links active
- **Verification:** Discord active daily with user-generated discussions; subreddit has 5+ weekly posts

## Week 3-4: App Store Optimization & Influencer Outreach

### Task 2.1 - App Store Listing Optimization (Google Play & Apple App Store)
- **Current state:** Store listings incomplete or not published
- **Goal:** Compelling, conversion-optimized listings on both platforms
- **Steps:**
  1. **Store Screenshots & Preview Video:**
     - Create 5-8 screenshots per platform showing: main menu, card selection, battle system, skins, progression
     - Captions on each screenshot highlighting key features ("Build Your Deck," "Face Global Tournament," "Unlock 25+ Cards")
     - 30s preview video showing gameplay loop: start run → battle → card pull → end screen
  2. **App Title & Subtitle:**
     - Title: "Core Surge: Tower Defense Card Game"
     - Subtitle (iOS only): "Build, Battle, Climb the Ranks"
  3. **Description (App Store & Play Store):**
     - Lead: "Defend your tower. Master card combos. Climb the global tournament."
     - Highlight: 25 unique cards, 7 skins, cross-platform progression, offline play, no ads/energy system
     - Keywords: tower defense, card game, roguelike, strategy, casual, free-to-play
  4. **Keyword Strategy:**
     - Primary: tower defense, card game, strategy game, roguelike
     - Secondary: casual games, offline games, free games, puzzle game
     - Long-tail: best card games 2026, tower defense strategy, roguelike deck builder
  5. **Ratings & Reviews Management:**
     - Build in-app prompt for 5-star reviews (post-milestone: rank 10, 100 battles, tournament win)
     - Respond to negative reviews within 48 hours (acknowledge bug, promise fix in update)
  6. **Localization (Phase 1 - launch support):**
     - Translate title, subtitle, description to 3-5 languages (Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Simplified Chinese)
     - Use native speakers or professional translator
- **Deliverable:** Complete store listings on Google Play & App Store; 5-8 screenshots; 30s video; localized content
- **Verification:** Listings live and visible in search; screenshots render correctly; keywords indexed

### Task 2.2 - Influencer & Content Creator Outreach
- **Current state:** No formal partnership program
- **Goal:** Seed influential players to generate organic content and reach
- **Strategy:**
  - Tier 1 (Micro-influencers 10K-100K): 15-20 creators in tower defense / roguelike / card game space
  - Tier 2 (Mid-tier 100K-500K): 5-10 gaming streamers with strategy game audience
  - Tier 3 (Aspirational 500K+): 2-3 major creators (outreach but low success likelihood)
- **Steps:**
  1. Build influencer list (search YouTube, Twitch, TikTok for tower defense / card game keywords)
  2. Create one-pager: game hook (5-10 min gameplay loop), key features, press kit with assets
  3. Personalized outreach (not mass mail):
     - Email: 2-3 sentences, link to game, what you admire about their content
     - DM on Twitter/Discord: more casual, invite to beta channel
  4. Offer incentives:
     - Early access to new cards/skins (cosmetic advantage, not P2W)
     - Exclusive "Creator" skin or cosmetic badge in-game
     - Revenue share if applicable (TBD based on monetization model)
  5. Track content: bookmark all creator videos/streams; engage (like, comment, share)
  6. Post-content feedback: thank them, ask what made it fun/hard, iterate
- **Deliverable:** Outreach list of 20-30 creators; 10+ positive responses; 3-5 creator videos by end of Week 4
- **Verification:** Creator videos generate 5K+ cumulative views; at least 1 Twitch stream (100+ concurrent viewers)

## Week 5-6: Closed Beta → Open Beta Transition

### Task 3.1 - Beta Feedback Integration & Build #2
- **Current state:** 50-100 testers, active feedback channel
- **Goal:** Rapid iteration based on feedback; prepare for wider open beta
- **Steps:**
  1. Triage and prioritize feedback (daily standup with build team):
     - P0: Game-breaking bugs, progression locks, major balance issues
     - P1: Quality-of-life, UI clarity, card balance, tutorial improvements
     - P2: Nice-to-have features, cosmetics, future content hints
  2. Commit to top 3-5 P0 fixes and 5-10 P1 improvements per week
  3. Communicate roadmap to testers ("Here's what we're fixing based on YOUR feedback")
  4. Release updated build to TestFlight/Play Console every 5-7 days
  5. Measure impact: session length, retention, bug report volume post-patch
- **Deliverable:** 2+ updated builds; documented feedback changelog; tester testimonials collected
- **Verification:** Retention improves week-over-week; tester engagement remains 60%+ active

### Task 3.2 - Open Beta Launch (End of Week 5/Early Week 6)
- **Current state:** Closed beta stable, feedback processed
- **Goal:** Expand to 1K-5K players for broader testing and marketing validation
- **Steps:**
  1. Prepare marketing blast:
     - Twitter announcement: "Core Surge Open Beta is LIVE! Join 50+ testers building this tower defense card game."
     - Reddit post: r/gaming, r/iphone, r/androidgaming (communities, not spam)
     - Discord announcement: all relevant servers (with moderator permission)
     - Email friends list with direct download links
  2. Scale infrastructure:
     - Ensure Firebase can handle 5K concurrent users (load test Week 4)
     - Monitor crash rates, latency, database performance
  3. Establish support flow:
     - Sticky support thread in Discord (questions answered within 1 hour)
     - Bug report process (clear repro steps, screenshot)
  4. Track cohort metrics:
     - Day-1, day-7, day-30 retention (compare to closed beta)
     - Average session length, gem spend (if monetization live)
     - Top churn points (which cards/ranks frustrate players)
- **Deliverable:** Open beta live on TestFlight, Play Console, and web (if applicable); 1K+ signups in first week
- **Verification:** Sustained 30%+ day-7 retention; 100+ organic social mentions (Reddit/Twitter); <1% crash rate

### Task 3.3 - Media Outreach & Press Kit
- **Current state:** No press relationships
- **Goal:** Secure coverage in gaming blogs, podcasts, YouTube channels
- **Steps:**
  1. Create press kit (PDF):
     - Game description (2-3 sentences)
     - Key features bullet list
     - Screenshots (high-res, 3-5 images)
     - Developer bio
     - Download links (TestFlight/Play Console)
  2. Identify press targets:
     - Gaming blogs: TouchArcade, Pocket Gamer, Kotaku, Game Informer
     - Podcasts: Game design-focused (Design Doc, Game Dev Radio)
     - YouTube channels: Indie game showcases, strategy game reviews
  3. Outreach (Week 5):
     - Personalized pitch emails (not mass mail): "We've built a tower defense card game with [unique hook]. Thought you'd find it interesting given your recent coverage of [similar game]."
     - Link to press kit, download links
  4. Provide early access to press (week before open beta launch)
  5. Track coverage: aggregate all published articles/videos
- **Deliverable:** Press kit created; 15+ media outlets contacted; 3-5 press articles/videos by Week 6
- **Verification:** Articles published on reputable gaming sites; cumulative reach 50K+ impressions

## Week 7: Open Beta Stabilization & Launch Prep

### Task 4.1 - Sustained Player Growth & Engagement
- **Current state:** 1K-5K open beta players; initial retention baseline established
- **Goal:** Maintain momentum; refine retention mechanics based on data
- **Steps:**
  1. Weekly feature updates (cosmetics, balance patches):
     - New cosmetic skin or card variant every 1-2 weeks (keeps content calendar fresh)
     - Balance changes based on card pull rates, rank progression speed
  2. In-game events (if time permits):
     - Limited-time tournament (1 week event, leaderboard ranking)
     - Double gem weekend (incentive to play, test monetization)
  3. Community events:
     - Weekly Discord trivia (cosmetic prizes)
     - Monthly art contest for fan card designs
     - Creator spotlight (feature a community player/streamer each week)
  4. Analytics-driven decisions:
     - Identify churn bottlenecks (which rank/card causes players to quit?)
     - A/B test tutorial flow, difficulty curve
     - Monitor gem spend distribution (whale vs. casual players)
- **Deliverable:** 2+ content updates; 1+ in-game event; weekly community engagement posts
- **Verification:** Week-7 DAU/MAU growth 10%+ week-over-week; retention stable or improving

     - [ ] Social media assets prepared (banners, GIFs)
     - [ ] Influencer partnerships confirmed (content calendar)

  4. **Operations readiness:**

     - [ ] Moderation team trained (Discord, in-game chat)

- **Deliverable:** Launch readiness checklist 100% complete; sign-off from all three teams
- **Verification:** All three task documents (BUILD, COMPLIANCE, LAUNCH) have green lights

## Week 8: Production Launch & Post-Launch Support

### Task 5.1 - Launch Day Operations
- **Current state:** Open beta stable; production systems ready
- **Goal:** Smooth transition from open beta to production launch
- **Steps:**
  1. **Pre-launch (24 hours before):**
     - Final database backup
     - Monitor Firebase quotas (ensure scaling active)
     - Alert team (Slack/email with launch timeline)
     - Finalize launch announcement (Tweet, Reddit, Discord pins)
  2. **Launch day (go live):**
     - Post announcements across all channels (Twitter, Reddit, Discord, blog)
     - Monitor crash rates, backend latency, error logs in real-time
     - Team on standby for critical bug fixes (P0 only)
     - Engage with new player questions (Discord support priority)
  3. **First 48 hours:**
     - Track player acquisition (signups, installs, DAU)
     - Monitor key metrics: retention, session length, crash rate
     - Rapidly patch any P0 bugs (priority over new features)
     - Celebrate milestones publicly (1K players, 5K players)
  4. **First week:**
     - Daily standups (10 min check-in: metrics, issues, support load)
     - Minor balance patches if data shows obvious problems
     - Community spotlight posts (feature interesting player runs, Discord conversations)
- **Deliverable:** Production launch live; player data flowing to analytics; support system operational
- **Verification:** 10K+ downloads in first 48 hours; <2% crash rate; 30%+ day-1 retention

### Task 5.2 - Post-Launch Content Roadmap (8-16 Weeks)
- **Current state:** Launch day; baseline player cohort established
- **Goal:** Sustained engagement through planned content drops and seasonal events
- **Content Calendar (Weeks 8-16):**
  - **Week 8 (Launch):** Core game launch, baseline features
  - **Week 9:** New card (26th unique card design, art, balance)
  - **Week 10:** First seasonal event (e.g., "Tower Defense Tournament," limited-time rules, cosmetic rewards)
  - **Week 11:** New skin variant (8th skin, 4 backgrounds, free + paid cosmetic option)
  - **Week 12:** Balance patch based on telemetry (card adjustments, rank scaling, gem economy)
  - **Week 13:** Influencer-driven event (teaser from partnered creator)
  - **Week 14:** Community-voted feature (poll top 3 QoL improvements, implement winner)
  - **Week 15:** Major holiday event (cosmetics, limited cards, special rules)
  - **Week 16:** Roadmap announcement (next quarter's content, player wish-list incorporated)
- **Success metrics per release:**
  - Engagement lift: +15% DAU on content release day, sustained at +5% for 1 week
  - Monetization: 10% of active players purchase cosmetics or battle pass (if applicable)
  - Community: 30+ discussions per release on Reddit/Discord
- **Deliverable:** Published 8-week roadmap; community alignment on priorities
- **Verification:** Content shipped on schedule; retention curves show positive lifts post-release

### Task 5.3 - Analytics & Community Reporting
- **Current state:** Analytics dashboard live
- **Goal:** Weekly transparent reporting to build trust and accountability
- **Steps:**
  1. **Weekly community report:**
     - DAU/MAU trends, retention, session length
     - Top 10 cards by pick rate, win rate
     - Tier balance (% players in each rank)
     - Churn analysis (which cards/ranks lose players fastest)
     - Most common bugs reported, resolution status
  2. **Public dashboard (optional):**
     - Embed analytics dashboard in-game or on website
     - Let community see meta-game trends in real-time
  3. **Quarterly financial transparency (optional, if P2P model):**
     - Revenue trends, player acquisition cost, lifetime value
     - Spending distribution (whale vs. casual)
     - Reinvestment plans (new content budget)
- **Deliverable:** First weekly report published (Week 8); dashboard setup (if chosen)
- **Verification:** Community trust metric (sentiment on Reddit/Discord shifts positive); sustained player base

- [ ] 1K-5K open beta players by Week 5
- [ ] 3-5 creator videos published before launch
- [ ] 5+ press articles published
- [ ] App store listings optimized and live

- [ ] <2% crash rate maintained post-launch

## Dependencies
- **Blockers:** Build pipeline must deliver playable APK/IPA by Week 6; compliance sign-off by Week 6
- **Parallel tracks:** Runs simultaneously with BUILD_PIPELINE_IMPLEMENTATION and COMPLIANCE_SECURITY_ANALYTICS
- **External dependencies:** 
  - TestFlight approval (24-48 hours)
  - Google Play Console approval (24-48 hours)
  - App Store review (1-3 days typical)
  - Influencer response time (1-2 weeks typical)
  - Press publication timeline (varies, 1-4 weeks)

---

## Timeline Risk Factors
- Influencer outreach (non-response common; plan for low conversion, cast wide net)
- App store review rejection (ensure compliance sign-off before submission)
- Day-1 player acquisition below target (validate marketing channels in beta)
- Retention cliff at rank 10 or card-specific progression (watch analytics daily, prepare backup balance changes)
- Community moderation (toxic players; have moderation guidelines + team ready by Week 6)
- Server scaling (load test at 5K players before launch; prepare auto-scaling config)

---

## Notes for Launch Team
- **Community first:** Players will drive success through word-of-mouth. Respond to feedback quickly, celebrate milestones publicly.
- **Data-driven iteration:** Use analytics to inform balance, marketing spend, content priorities. Don't guess.
- **Transparency:** Show the roadmap, explain decisions, admit mistakes. Builds trust and long-term retention.
- **Content velocity:** Every 1-2 weeks, players should see *something* new (cosmetic, card, event, balance patch). Prevents staleness.
