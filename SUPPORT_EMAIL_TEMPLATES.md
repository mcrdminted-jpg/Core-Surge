## Template 1: Lost Progress

**Subject:** Re: Lost Game Progress - Core Surge Support

Hi [PLAYER_NAME],

Thank you for reaching out. I understand losing your progress is frustrating, and I want to help get you back on track.

Here are a few things to check:

1. If you were playing as a guest (without creating an account), your progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data will erase it. Unfortunately, guest progress cannot be recovered once deleted.

2. If you have a registered account (email/password), your progress should be saved to the cloud. Try signing out and signing back in from Settings > Cloud Save.

3. If you switched devices, make sure you are signed into the same account on the new device, then tap "Load Cloud Save" in Settings.

If none of these steps resolve the issue, please reply with:
- Your Player ID (found in Settings > Profile)
- The device you were playing on
- Whether you had a registered account or were playing as a guest
- Approximately how far you had progressed (tier, wave, cards collected)

I will investigate further and do everything I can to help.

Best,
Core Surge Support Team

## Template 3: Subscription Cancellation

**Subject:** Re: Cancel Monthly Vault Subscription - Core Surge Support

Hi [PLAYER_NAME],

Thank you for reaching out. Subscriptions for Core Surge are managed through your device's app store, not through us directly.

To cancel your Monthly Vault subscription:

**On iPhone/iPad:**
1. Open the Settings app on your device
2. Tap your name at the top
3. Tap Subscriptions
4. Find "Core Surge" and tap it
5. Tap Cancel Subscription

**On Android:**
1. Open the Google Play Store app
2. Tap your profile icon at the top right
3. Tap Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions
4. Find "Core Surge" and tap it
5. Tap Cancel subscription

After cancelling, you will continue to receive daily gems until the end of your current billing period. No further charges will occur.

If you have already cancelled but are still being charged, please contact Apple Support or Google Play Support directly, as they process all subscription payments.

Best,
Core Surge Support Team

## Template 6: Refund Request

**Subject:** Re: Refund Request - Core Surge Support

Hi [PLAYER_NAME],

Thank you for reaching out about a refund.

Refunds for in-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google, not by us directly. To request a refund:

**Apple (iOS):**
Visit https://reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Core Surge purchase, and submit a refund request.

**Google (Android):**
Open the Google Play Store, tap your profile icon, go to Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history, find the purchase, and tap "Request a refund."

Both Apple and Google typically process refund requests within 48 hours. Refund eligibility is determined by their respective policies.

If you are experiencing a technical issue that prevented you from receiving your purchase, please let me know and I can investigate on our end first.

Best,
Core Surge Support Team
