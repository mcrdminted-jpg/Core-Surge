# Refund Policy

## Core Surge: Endless Tower Defense

**Last Updated:** 2026-05-24
**Support Contact:** support@coresurge.game
**Response Time:** Within 48 hours for purchase issues

---

## General Policy

Core Surge is a free-to-play game with optional in-app purchases. All purchases are processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not process payments or refunds directly.

---

## Platform Refunds

### Apple App Store

- Refund requests are submitted by the player through Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- Apple reviews and decides whether to grant the refund
- We receive a notification from Apple when a refund is processed
- We do not have the ability to approve or deny refunds on Apple's platform

### Google Play Store

- Refund requests are submitted by the player through Google Play or by contacting Google support
- Google Play offers automatic refunds within 48 hours of purchase (for most transactions)
- After 48 hours, Google reviews refund requests on a case-by-case basis
- We receive a notification from Google when a refund is processed

---

## Virtual Currency (Gems)

- Gems are a consumable virtual currency used within Core Surge
- Gems are non-refundable once delivered to the player's account
- Gems have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash
- Gems cannot be transferred between accounts

---

## Refund Consequences

When a refund is processed by Apple or Google:

1. We receive a webhook/server notification of the refund
2. The gem amount from the refunded purchase is deducted from the player's account
3. If the player has already spent some or all of the refunded gems:
   - Gem balance goes negative (player must earn or purchase gems to return to positive)
   - Items/cards obtained with those gems are NOT revoked (to avoid disrupting gameplay)
   - The player cannot make new purchases until their gem balance is positive
4. If the refund involves a subscription (Monthly Vault):
   - Subscription benefits are immediately revoked
   - Any gems received from the subscription during the refunded period are deducted

---

## Double-Charge Protection

If a player reports being charged twice for the same purchase:

1. Player contacts support@coresurge.game with:
   - Their player ID or account email
   - Screenshot of the duplicate charges
   - Transaction IDs if available
2. We verify the charges in RevenueCat dashboard
3. If a duplicate charge is confirmed:
   - We direct the player to request a refund for the duplicate through the platform (Apple/Google)
   - We ensure only one set of gems was delivered
4. Resolution target: within 48 hours of report

---

## Subscription (Monthly Vault)

- The Monthly Vault subscription can be cancelled at any time through the player's device settings (App Store Subscriptions or Google Play Subscriptions)
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
- The player retains subscription benefits until the end of the paid period
- No prorated refunds are issued for partial billing periods
- Gems and rewards already received from the subscription are not revoked upon cancellation (only upon refund)
- Players can re-subscribe at any time

---

## Defective or Missing Purchases

If a purchase completes (player is charged) but gems or items are not delivered:

1. Player contacts support@coresurge.game with:
   - Their player ID or account email
   - Purchase receipt or transaction ID
   - Description of what was expected vs. what was received
2. We verify the transaction in RevenueCat and our server logs
3. If the purchase is confirmed but items were not delivered:
   - We manually credit the gems or items to the player's account
   - We investigate and fix the delivery issue to prevent recurrence
4. If the purchase cannot be verified:
   - We direct the player to contact Apple/Google support with their receipt
5. Resolution target: within 48 hours of report

---

## Fraud Prevention

We monitor for refund abuse to protect the game economy and fair play:

- **Flag threshold:** Accounts with more than 2 refunds within a 6-month period are flagged for review
- **Review process:** Flagged accounts are manually reviewed to determine if the refunds are legitimate
- **Consequences for abuse:**
  - First offense: Warning sent to the player's email
  - Second offense: Account restricted from making purchases for 30 days
  - Third offense: Account permanently restricted from purchases; gameplay access retained
- **Chargeback fraud:** If a player initiates a chargeback (dispute with bank/credit card) instead of a platform refund, the account may be immediately suspended pending review
- We reserve the right to suspend accounts engaged in purchase-refund cycling (buying gems, spending them, then requesting refunds)
