# App Store Screenshot Specifications

## Required Sizes

### Apple App Store

| Device | Resolution | Aspect Ratio | Required |
|--------|-----------|--------------|----------|
| iPhone 6.7" (Pro Max) | 1290 x 2796 | 9:19.5 | Yes - Primary display |
| iPhone 6.1" (Standard) | 1179 x 2556 | 9:19.5 | Yes |
| iPhone 5.5" (Plus/Legacy) | 1242 x 2208 | 9:16 | Yes - Older device support |
| iPad 12.9" (Pro) | 2048 x 2732 | 3:4 | Yes if supporting iPad |

### Google Play Store

| Device | Resolution | Required |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Phone | 1080 x 1920 minimum | Yes |
| Tablet | 1920 x 1200 | Optional |
| Chromebook | 1920 x 1080 | Optional |

## Screenshot Content Plan (5-8 per platform)

All screenshots use the same content across platforms, resized to each required dimension. Maintain consistent branding, font, and layout proportions when scaling.

### Screenshot 1: Battle Scene
- **Headline:** DEFEND THE CORE
- **Content:** Active battle with enemies approaching, projectiles in flight, damage numbers visible
- **Focus:** Show the intensity of wave combat with multiple enemy types on screen
- **Visual priority:** Tower centered, enemies surrounding, particle effects active

### Screenshot 2: Research/Upgrade Screen
- **Headline:** UNLOCK POWERFUL ABILITIES
- **Content:** Research tree or upgrade family screen showing progression paths
- **Focus:** Display the depth of the upgrade system with multiple families visible
- **Visual priority:** Show partially unlocked tree to convey progression

### Screenshot 3: Card Collection
- **Headline:** COLLECT 25 UNIQUE CARDS
- **Content:** Card collection or loadout screen with cards of different rarities visible
- **Focus:** Show variety of cards with Standard, Prime, and Apex rarity visual differences
- **Visual priority:** Fan or grid of cards with rarity glow effects

### Screenshot 4: Home Screen / Tier Selector
- **Headline:** CONQUER 18 DIFFICULTY TIERS
- **Content:** Home screen showing tier selection with progression indicators
- **Focus:** Convey the long-term challenge and progression depth
- **Visual priority:** Tier list with some locked, some unlocked, current tier highlighted

### Screenshot 5: Tournament Leaderboard
- **Headline:** COMPETE GLOBALLY
- **Content:** Tournament screen showing leaderboard with league badges
- **Focus:** Show competitive element and league structure
- **Visual priority:** Leaderboard entries, league icons, reward previews

### Screenshot 6: Skin Gallery
- **Headline:** CUSTOMIZE YOUR EXPERIENCE
- **Content:** Skin selection screen showing multiple tower/background themes
- **Focus:** Visual variety and cosmetic appeal
- **Visual priority:** Side-by-side or carousel of different skin styles

### Screenshot 7: Shop / Strategic Upgrades
- **Headline:** STRATEGIC UPGRADES
- **Content:** Shop or upgrade decision screen showing meaningful choices
- **Focus:** Convey strategic depth without looking pay-to-win
- **Visual priority:** Upgrade options with clear stat descriptions

### Screenshot 8: Battle HUD Close-up
- **Headline:** DEEP STRATEGIC COMBAT
- **Content:** Close-up of battle UI showing combat stats, card abilities active, combo indicators
- **Focus:** Detail and polish of the combat system
- **Visual priority:** Active card effects, damage stats, wave counter

## Design Guidelines

- All screenshots in portrait orientation
- Dark background consistent with game aesthetic (#08101c base)
- Headline text: Bold white, positioned top or bottom with safe margin
- No device frames in the screenshots themselves (stores add these)
- Minimum 5 screenshots per platform, maximum 8
- First screenshot is most important (shown in search results)
- Avoid text smaller than the App Store minimum readable size
