# Support FAQ

## Core Surge: Endless Tower Defense

**Last Updated:** 2026-05-25

---

## Getting Started

**Q: How do I play Core Surge?**
Tap "Battle" on the home screen to start a run. Your tower automatically attacks enemies as they approach. Use Scrap earned from kills to buy temporary in-run upgrades. Survive as many waves as possible. When your Core HP reaches 0, the run ends and you earn Scrap based on performance. Spend Scrap between runs on permanent upgrades in the Research tab.

**Q: Do I need an internet connection to play?**
No. Core Surge works fully offline for basic gameplay. Internet is required for cloud saves, tournament leaderboards, and in-app purchases.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
No. You can play as a guest. However, creating an account (free) enables cloud saves so your progress syncs across devices and is backed up if you switch phones.

## Progress & Saves

**Q: How do I save my progress?**
Your progress saves automatically to your device after every run. If you have a registered account, it also syncs to the cloud.

**Q: I lost my progress. Can you recover it?**
If you had a registered account, sign in on your device and tap "Load Cloud Save" in Settings. Guest progress is stored locally only and cannot be recovered if the app is uninstalled or data is cleared.

**Q: How do I transfer progress to a new device?**
Create an account (Settings > Cloud Save > Register), ensure your progress is synced, then sign into the same account on your new device and load your cloud save.

**Q: What happens if I uninstall the app?**
If you have a registered account, your progress is safe in the cloud. Reinstall and sign in to restore it. Guest progress will be lost.

## Cards & Gems

**Q: How do I get cards?**
Cards are pulled using gems. Go to the Cards tab and tap "Pull Card" to spend gems for a random card.

**Q: What are the card odds?**
Standard cards: 78% chance per pull. Prime cards: 20%. Apex cards: 2%. Full odds for all 25 cards are viewable in-game.

**Q: How do I level up cards?**
Collect duplicate copies of a card. Duplicates automatically merge to increase the card's level (max level 5).

**Q: How do I get gems without paying?**
Gems can be earned by collecting gem orbs during battle (they appear as rare spawns), reaching milestone wave targets, placing well in tournaments, and watching rewarded video ads.

## Purchases & Subscriptions

**Q: What can I buy?**
Four products are available: Starter Pack (one-time bundle), Small Gem Pack, Medium Gem Pack, and Monthly Vault (subscription for daily gems).

**Q: Is Core Surge pay-to-win?**
No. All gameplay content is achievable through regular play. Gem purchases accelerate progression but do not unlock exclusive power or content.

**Q: How do I cancel my Monthly Vault subscription?**
Cancel through your device's subscription settings (Apple: Settings > Your Name > Subscriptions; Android: Play Store > Subscriptions). You keep daily gems until the billing period ends.

**Q: How do I request a refund?**
Refunds are processed by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google (Play Store > Payments > History). We do not process refunds directly.

**Q: I purchased gems but did not receive them.**
Try closing and reopening the app. Then go to Settings > Billing > Restore Purchases. If gems still do not appear, contact us with your Player ID and purchase receipt.

## Tournaments

**Q: How do tournaments work?**
Tournaments run on timed windows. Your best wave reached during the tournament period determines your rank. Top players are promoted to higher leagues; bottom players are demoted.

**Q: How many tournament tiers are there?**
18 tiers (matching game tiers T1-T18), each with 5 leagues.

**Q: What are the tournament rewards?**
Gems and bonus Scrap based on your final placement within your league.

## Technical Issues

**Q: The app crashes or freezes.**
Try closing the app completely and reopening it. Make sure you are running the latest version. If the problem continues, contact us with your device model, OS version, and a description of when the crash happens.

**Q: Ads are not loading.**
Ad availability depends on your region and network connection. This does not affect gameplay. Rewarded ad bonuses will be available when ads load successfully.

**Q: The game runs slowly.**
Close other apps running in the background. Core Surge is optimized for most devices, but older devices may experience slowdowns during high-wave battles with many enemies on screen.

## Account & Privacy

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
Go to Settings > Profile > Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. Active subscriptions must be cancelled separately through your app store.

**Q: What data do you collect?**
We collect your email (if registered), display name, game progress, and purchase history. We do not sell or share your data for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

**Q: Is Core Surge safe for kids?**
Core Surge is rated 13+. Users under 13 are not permitted to create accounts. The game contains fantasy violence and a card pull system with randomized rewards.
