# Community Guidelines

## Core Surge: Endless Tower Defense

**Last Updated:** 2026-05-24
**Platforms:** Discord Server, Reddit Community (r/CoreSurge)

---

## Welcome

Welcome to the Core Surge community. These guidelines exist to maintain a positive, inclusive, and enjoyable space for all players. By participating in our Discord server or Reddit community, you agree to follow these rules.

---

## General Conduct

1. **Be respectful.** Treat all community members with courtesy and respect, even during disagreements.
2. **No harassment.** Do not target, bully, stalk, or repeatedly antagonize other members.
3. **No hate speech.** Zero tolerance for racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, or any form of discrimination.
4. **No threats.** Do not threaten violence, doxxing, or any form of real-world harm.
5. **No trolling.** Do not post inflammatory content solely to provoke reactions.
6. **Keep it civil.** Debates about game mechanics and strategy are encouraged; personal attacks are not.

---

## Username Policy

- No offensive, vulgar, or sexually explicit usernames
- No impersonation of developers, moderators, public figures, or other players
- No misleading names that imply official status (e.g., "CoreSurge_Admin", "Official_Dev")
- Moderators may request a username change; failure to comply may result in a ban

---

## Content Rules

- **No NSFW content.** No pornography, gore, or graphic violence in any form (text, images, links, emojis)
- **No real-money trading.** Do not buy, sell, or trade game accounts, items, or currency for real money
- **No account sharing.** Do not share or request access to other players' accounts
- **No advertising.** Do not promote other games, products, services, or Discord servers without moderator permission
- **No spam.** Do not flood channels with repetitive messages, excessive caps, or meaningless content
- **No piracy.** Do not share or request pirated software, hacked APKs, or modified game clients
- **No cheating discussion.** Do not share exploits, hacks, or methods to cheat. Report them to moderators privately instead.

---

## Spoiler Policy

- Use spoiler tags for unreleased content, datamined information, and leaked details
- Discord: Use `||spoiler text||` formatting
- Reddit: Use the built-in spoiler tag feature
- Do not put spoilers in post titles or channel topic lines
- Content from official announcements and patch notes is not considered a spoiler

---

## Bug Reporting

Use the designated `#bug-reports` channel (Discord) or Bug Report flair (Reddit).

**Required information:**
- Device model and OS version (e.g., iPhone 15, iOS 18.2)
- Game version number (found in Settings)
- Steps to reproduce the bug
- What you expected to happen vs. what actually happened
- Screenshots or video if possible

**Do not:**
- Report bugs in general chat (they get lost)
- Report the same bug that has already been reported (check pinned messages first)
- Demand immediate fixes (the dev team is small and prioritizes based on severity)

---

## Suggestions and Feedback

Use the designated `#suggestions` channel (Discord) or Suggestion flair (Reddit).

- Search before posting to avoid duplicates
- One suggestion per post for clarity
- Be constructive: explain the problem your suggestion solves
- Upvote existing suggestions you agree with instead of creating duplicates
- The dev team reads all suggestions but cannot respond to or implement every one

---

## Moderation

### Enforcement Ladder

| Step | Action | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verbal warning | Immediate |
| 2 | Mute | 24 hours |
| 3 | Temporary ban | 7 days |
| 4 | Permanent ban | Indefinite |

- Severe violations (hate speech, threats, doxxing, NSFW) may skip directly to a temporary or permanent ban
- Moderation actions are logged and reviewed by the moderation team
- Moderators have final say on rule interpretation and enforcement

### Appeals

- To appeal a moderation action, DM a moderator (do not post appeals in public channels)
- Include: your username, the action taken, and why you believe it should be reconsidered
- Appeals are reviewed within 48 hours
- Appeal decisions are final
- Do not harass moderators about pending appeals

---

## Content Creator Program

### Eligibility

- Active community member in good standing
- Regularly produces Core Surge content (YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, etc.)
- Minimum audience: 100 subscribers/followers (flexible based on content quality)

### Benefits

- Verified Creator role on Discord
- Early access to patch notes (under NDA, typically 24-48 hours before public release)
- Permission to monetize Core Surge content (videos, streams, guides)
- Direct feedback channel with the development team
- Consideration for featuring on official Core Surge social media

### Requirements

- Follow all community guidelines
- Clearly disclose sponsored content
- Do not share NDA-protected information before the embargo lifts
- Maintain activity (at least 1 piece of Core Surge content per month)

### Application

- Contact a moderator or email support@coresurge.game with links to your content

## Giveaway Rules

- Only official giveaways (run by developers or authorized moderators) are permitted
- Do not ask for free gems, items, or accounts
- Do not run unauthorized giveaways or raffles
- Official giveaway rules will be posted with each event
- Giveaway winners are selected fairly (random draw or contest-based as specified)

---

## Bot Usage

- Only approved bots are allowed on the Discord server
- Current approved bots: MEE6 (moderation and leveling)
- Do not add unauthorized bots to the server
- Do not spam bot commands in non-designated channels
- Bot abuse (exploiting leveling, spamming commands) results in moderation action

---

## Language

- **Primary language:** English
- All official channels are English-only
- Additional language-specific channels may be created based on community demand and available moderation
- Current additional language channels: None (will be added as the community grows)
- Non-English channels still follow all community guidelines

---

## Reporting Violations

- **Discord:** Use the `@Moderator` ping in the relevant channel, or DM a moderator directly
- **Reddit:** Use the Report button on the offending post/comment
- **Email:** support@coresurge.game for serious issues
- Include screenshots or evidence when reporting
- Do not publicly call out rule breakers; let moderators handle it

---

## Changes to Guidelines

These guidelines may be updated as the community grows. Changes will be announced in the `#announcements` channel on Discord and pinned on the subreddit. Continued participation after guideline updates constitutes acceptance of the new rules.
