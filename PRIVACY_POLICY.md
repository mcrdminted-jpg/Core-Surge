# Privacy Policy

**Core Surge: Endless Tower Defense**

**Effective Date:** May 24, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Andrew Evans Anglin ("Developer," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the mobile game "Core Surge: Endless Tower Defense" ("Game," "App," or "Service"). By downloading, installing, or using the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Username (Display Name):** A player-chosen display name used for leaderboards, tournaments, and in-game identification. This is not required to be your real name.
- **Email Address:** Collected only if you create an account via Firebase Authentication. Account creation is optional.
- **Firebase Authentication UID:** A unique identifier generated when you create an account.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Play Statistics:** Waves completed, damage dealt, Scrap earned, gems earned, cards collected, tournament results, and other gameplay metrics.
- **Device Identifier:** A device-level identifier used for analytics and crash reporting purposes.
- **Usage Data:** How you interact with the Game, including session duration, features used, and in-app purchase activity.
- **Crash and Performance Data:** Technical information about crashes, errors, and performance metrics collected via Firebase Crashlytics.
- **Advertising Data:** Ad interaction data including impressions, clicks, and ad identifiers, collected by Google AdMob.

### 1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

- **Firebase Authentication:** Email address, UID, and account metadata for account management.
- **Firebase Firestore:** Cloud storage of player profiles, game saves, and tournament data.
- **Firebase Analytics:** Anonymized usage events and engagement metrics.
- **Firebase Crashlytics:** Crash reports including device model, OS version, and stack traces.
- **RevenueCat:** Purchase management, transaction receipts, and entitlement tracking.
- **Google AdMob:** Advertising delivery, ad interaction data, and advertising identifiers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **Game Functionality:** Saving and syncing your game progress across devices.
- **Tournament Matchmaking:** Matching players for competitive tournament play based on performance data.
- **Analytics:** Understanding how players interact with the Game to improve gameplay and balance.
- **Crash Reporting:** Identifying and fixing technical issues and crashes.
- **Advertising:** Serving advertisements within the Game, including personalized ads (unless you opt out).
- **Purchase Management:** Processing and verifying in-app purchases, managing entitlements.
- **Communication:** Responding to support requests sent to our contact email.

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following circumstances:

- **Third-Party Service Providers:** With the third-party services listed in Section 1.3, solely for the purposes described in this policy.
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
- **Protection of Rights:** To protect the rights, property, or safety of the Developer, our users, or others.
- **Aggregated or Anonymized Data:** We may share aggregated or anonymized information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

## 4. Data Retention

- **Active Account Data:** Your player profile, game saves, and account information are retained while your account remains active.
- **Deletion Requests:** Upon receiving a valid deletion request, we will delete your personal data within 30 days.
- **Analytics Data:** Anonymized analytics data is retained per Google's default retention policy (14 months).
- **Crash Reports:** Crash report data is retained for 90 days.
- **Purchase Records:** Purchase receipts may be retained as required for tax and audit purposes, even after account deletion.

## 6. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Right to Access:** You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Erasure:** You may request that we delete your personal data.
- **Right to Data Portability:** You may request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON).
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** You may request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
- **Right to Object:** You may object to the processing of your personal data, including automated processing.
- **Right to Rectification:** You may request that we correct inaccurate personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@coresurge.game. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

**Legal Basis for Processing:** We process your data based on:
- **Contractual Necessity:** To provide the Game and its features.
- **Legitimate Interest:** For analytics, crash reporting, and game improvement.
- **Consent:** For personalized advertising and optional account creation.
