# Store Category Selection

## Primary Category: Games > Strategy

### Justification
Core Surge is fundamentally a strategy game. Players must make meaningful decisions across multiple systems:
- **Loadout strategy:** Selecting which of 25 cards to equip and how to combine their effects
- **Upgrade investment:** Choosing which of 6 upgrade families to prioritize with limited resources
- **Resource management:** Allocating gems between card pulls, upgrades, and cosmetics
- **Tier progression:** Deciding when to push to higher tiers versus farming current tiers for resources
- **Tournament play:** Optimizing loadouts for competitive scoring rather than survival

### Competitor Landscape in Strategy
- Bloons TD 6 (tower defense, strategy)
- Kingdom Rush series (tower defense, strategy)
- The Tower (idle tower defense, strategy)
- Arknights (tower defense, strategy/RPG)
- Random Dice (tower defense, PvP strategy)

Core Surge fits naturally alongside these titles in player expectations and search behavior.

## Secondary Category: Games > Action

### Justification (Apple allows a secondary category)
- Real-time combat with active projectile mechanics (crits, multishot, bounce)
- Enemies approach in real time requiring awareness and reactive card usage
- Battle pacing creates action-game tension during wave combat
- Visual spectacle of combat effects appeals to action game audiences

## Why NOT Casual
- 18 difficulty tiers with steep progression curve targets dedicated players
- 25 cards with leveling and rarity systems create midcore collection depth
- Tournament system with leagues adds competitive pressure
- 6 upgrade families with 20+ stats require long-term investment
- Target audience is midcore strategy/defense players, not casual pick-up-and-play

## Apple Keywords (100 chars max)
```
tower defense,idle,endless,upgrade,cards,gacha,strategy,defense game,offline,tournament
```

### Keyword Strategy
- "tower defense" - primary genre, highest intent
- "idle" - captures idle game searchers, Core Surge has idle-adjacent progression
- "endless" - describes the core gameplay loop
- "upgrade" - key feature players search for
- "cards" - collectible card mechanic is a strong draw
- "gacha" - card pull system matches gacha audience expectations
- "strategy" - category reinforcement
- "defense game" - alternate phrasing of genre
- "offline" - high-value differentiator
- "tournament" - competitive feature differentiator
