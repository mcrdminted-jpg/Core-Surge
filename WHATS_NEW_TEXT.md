# What's New Text

Release notes displayed in both Apple App Store and Google Play Store. Keep entries concise and player-facing. Avoid internal jargon.

---

## v1.0.0 - Launch

Welcome to Core Surge! Command your tower against endless waves of enemies.

Features:
- 25 collectible cards across 3 rarities (Standard, Prime, Apex)
- 6 upgrade families with 20+ permanent stats
- 18 difficulty tiers to conquer
- Tournament system with 5 leagues per tier
- 7 cosmetic skins with custom backgrounds and tower designs
- Cloud save support
- Offline play

Good luck, Commander!

## Writing Guidelines

- Maximum 500 characters for Apple (iOS 16+), 500 characters for Google Play
- Lead with the most exciting change
- Use bullet points for readability
- Mention new content before bug fixes
- Avoid version numbers in the body text (the store displays these separately)
- Keep tone consistent: direct, confident, player-focused
