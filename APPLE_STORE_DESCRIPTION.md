## Full Description

One tower. Endless enemies. Infinite strategy.

Core Surge is a single-tower endless defense game where every decision matters. You command the Core, a towering weapon platform under siege by relentless waves of enemies. Upgrade your arsenal, collect powerful cards, and push deeper into increasingly brutal difficulty tiers.

ENDLESS WAVE COMBAT
Face off against 12 distinct enemy types, each with unique behaviors and weaknesses. Waves never stop, and neither does the challenge. Every run tests your loadout, your upgrades, and your reflexes.

25 COLLECTIBLE CARDS
Build your strategy with 25 unique ability cards across three rarity tiers: Standard, Prime, and Apex. Each card changes how your Core fights. Combine cards into loadouts that match your playstyle, whether you favor raw damage, area control, or chain combos.

6 UPGRADE FAMILIES
Invest in permanent progression across six research families. Every rank-up makes your Core stronger for every future run. With 20+ stats to improve, your tower evolves into a uniquely powerful weapon over time.

18 DIFFICULTY TIERS
Progress through 18 escalating tiers of challenge. Each tier introduces tougher enemies, faster waves, and greater rewards. Reaching the highest tiers takes mastery of every system the game offers.

GLOBAL TOURNAMENTS
Compete against players worldwide in structured tournament seasons. Five leagues per tier let you climb the ranks, earn exclusive rewards, and prove your Core is the strongest.

7 COSMETIC SKINS
Customize your experience with seven distinct visual themes. Each skin transforms your tower, background, and effects with a unique aesthetic.

STRATEGIC DEPTH THAT REWARDS MASTERY
Unlike traditional tower defense games with dozens of towers to place, Core Surge distills the genre to its purest form. One tower, one battlefield, and an overwhelming number of strategic choices. Your card loadout, upgrade path, and in-battle decisions determine how far you go.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Play offline anytime. Save your progress to the cloud when you want. Jump into tournaments when you are ready to compete. Core Surge respects your time while rewarding dedication.

Download now and see how far your Core can survive.
